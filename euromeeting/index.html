Welcome to Savonlinna

Join us in the heart of Finland's lake district for 37-Euromeet 2027—an unforgettable experience surrounded by breathtaking nature, vibrant community, and unique Nordic charm.
Savonlinna is a city where lakes and nature are everywhere. In fact, you can take a refreshing dip in crystal-clear lake waters right in the city center—even in May!

Event Theme: Lakes & Nature

Experience the perfect blend of outdoor adventure, culture, and connection in one of Finland's most picturesque destinations. Enjoy events that combine fun with the beauty of our beloved city.

    • Organized by the event team
    • Requires sufficient participants
    • Price confirmed closer to the event. And will be an extra cost to the ticket holder
  3. 3. Train
    • From Helsinki Airport to Savonlinna city center (~2 transfers on a basic train trip)
    • You book your ticket (we’re happy to help if needed)
